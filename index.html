Limits

  • Models share training data, so agreement is partly manufactured. The shared-bias slider shows how little it takes to erase the gain.
  • The Bayes factors come from one illustrative benchmark, not measured data. Technology readiness level 1–2.
  • Agreement on retrieval questions carries no evidence (factor 1).
